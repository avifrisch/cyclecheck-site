Contact

If you're in crisis, don't wait for an email: call or text 988 (U.S.), or call 911.

Common questions

How do I share with a parent? On your iPhone, open CycleCheck → Settings → Share with a parent and send the invitation. Your parent taps the link on their iPhone, with CycleCheck installed, to accept. Use the same button to see who has access or to stop sharing.

Why didn't my parent get an alert? Alerts depend on iCloud delivering a background update, which iOS can delay. They don't arrive if CycleCheck has been swiped away in the app switcher on the parent's iPhone, or if alerts are off in the parent's Settings. Alerts are not an emergency service.

My periods from the Health app don't show up. Go to CycleCheck → Settings → Review Health access and allow CycleCheck to read menstrual flow.

I got a new iPhone. Sign in with the same iCloud account and install CycleCheck. Your check-ins are restored from iCloud.

How do I delete my data? Delete check-ins in the app, or delete the app. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
