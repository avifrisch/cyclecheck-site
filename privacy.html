CycleCheck Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Summary

CycleCheck is a daily symptom diary for premenstrual dysphoric disorder (PMDD). Your check-ins stay on your iPhone, in your own Apple Health data, and in your own iCloud account. We do not run servers, collect analytics, show ads, or sell or share data with anyone.

What CycleCheck stores

Daily check-ins (symptom ratings and optional notes), period days, and treatments (such as medications or supplements you choose to log, and the days you mark them taken). These are saved on your iPhone with iOS data protection and backed up to a private area of your own iCloud account through Apple CloudKit. Ratings, notes, your name, period days, and treatments are end-to-end encrypted in iCloud, so neither Apple nor the developer can read them. You can also require Face ID to open the app.

Migraine log. If you use the migraine log, CycleCheck stores each migraine you record (when it started and ended, how bad it was, symptoms, medicines and whether they helped, possible triggers, and notes). It is saved on your iPhone and backed up, end-to-end encrypted, to a separate private area of your own iCloud account. It is not included when you share with family.

Apple Health. With your permission, CycleCheck reads the menstrual flow data in Cycle Tracking. It also saves period days you log, and optionally symptoms such as mood changes, fatigue, and headache, to Health. Health data is never used for advertising or shared with third parties, and it is never stored outside your device and iCloud.

Apple Watch. Quick logs from the watch are sent directly to your paired iPhone.

Sharing with family

You can invite specific people, such as a parent, to view your check-ins, notes, period days, treatments, and report. Sharing uses Apple's iCloud sharing. Invited people can view your data but not change it, and you can see who has access or stop sharing at any time from Settings → Share with a parent.

Hard-day alerts. If you rate feeling hopeless, worthless, overwhelmed, or out of control as severe, the people you share with get a notification on their own device. iCloud only tells their device that something changed; their device reads your encrypted check-in and decides whether to show the alert. No server run by the developer is involved, and alerts can be turned off on the parent's device.

Children and teens

CycleCheck is meant to be used by teens and young people with a parent's involvement. Because the developer does not collect, receive, or store any personal information, no information about children is collected by the developer. A child's data stays on their device, in their Apple Health data, and in their own iCloud account, and is shared only with people they invite. Parents can contact us with any questions.

Doctor reports

PDF reports are created on your device and leave it only when you choose to share them.

Deleting your data

Delete individual check-ins in the app. Deleting the app removes data stored on the device. To remove iCloud data, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. Health data can be managed in the Health app.

Not medical advice

CycleCheck helps you keep a record to discuss with a clinician. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (U.S.) or call 911.
